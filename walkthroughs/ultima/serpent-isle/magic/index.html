Spells & ReagentsSerpent Isle

All 72 spells across nine circles, the reagents each one needs, and where to learn it: from Moonshade's mages, as scrolls, or for free from Gustacio after his lightning experiment.

Sources

Spell reagents and sources come from Mike's RPG Center (reagents), with reagent sellers and sources from Adrian Yau's walkthrough. Prices differ slightly between the two (e.g. Death Vortex 525 vs. 545); we show Mike's.

Spell scroll and reagent pictures come from the Ultima Codex (CC BY-SA 3.0), shown here enlarged. The artwork itself is © Origin Systems / EA.

Reagents

f = filari, m = monetari, g = guilders. Harnna's prices appear as "8/3" and "9/2" in Adrian Yau's guide, which probably means a price for a quantity. Check in play.
