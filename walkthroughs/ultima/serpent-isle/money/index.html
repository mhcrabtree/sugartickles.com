Treasure & MoneySerpent Isle

Every town on Serpent Isle mints its own coins, and shopkeepers only take their own. Gems, gold nuggets, jewelry, and gold bars have to go through a money changer first. Here's who takes what, what they pay, and where you get the best deal.

⚠️ Spoiler: two changers stop trading

Spektor is arrested partway through the game, and Zulith stops changing money once you catch him spying on you. Don't count on either for long. Devra and Bucia are there the whole game.

Tips

  • Raid the Royal Mint early. On the way through the Sleeping Bull chapter you reach the Royal Mint. Take as many gold bars as you can carry: each one is worth 200 monetari. See The Inn of the Sleeping Bull.
  • Keep one gold bar for Hawk. His fine at Bull Tower can be paid with a gold bar. Drop the rest first, or the pikemen ask for more.
  • Sell gems in Moonshade. Topo pays 100 guilders (33⅓ monetari) a gem, against Devra's 25 monetari. Hold your gems until you get there.
  • Trolls carry gems. The abandoned building east of Monitor's North Gate has trolls, experience, and gems. See Monitor, City of Courage.
  • Carry monetari. They're worth the most per coin, so a purse of monetari weighs least. Change into the local coin just before you shop.
  • Undertaker's bounty. In Monitor, Renfry pays 100 monetari a body for Johnson and the two slain guardsmen.
To verify while provingThe False Coin spell (2nd circle) is said to make counterfeit coins, and one guide advises using it only on monetari, the most valuable. Check how it works and whether shopkeepers ever notice. Also check whether the changers' fees apply when you sell treasure or only when you change coins.
One test takes your moneyOne of the tests in the Temple of Ethicality asks you to set down all your money, gold, and gems. Spend or stash what you can before you go. See The Temple of Ethicality.
