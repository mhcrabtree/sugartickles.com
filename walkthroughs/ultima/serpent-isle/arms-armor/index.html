Weapons & ArmorSerpent Isle

What each weapon hits for and what each piece of armor blocks, with a picture of every item so you can match it to what's in your pack. Armor also shows warmth, which matters once you head north.

Sources

Weapon, ammunition and armor pictures and values come from StrategyWiki's Serpent Isle Weapons and Armor pages (CC BY-SA 4.0). Warmth values, shop prices and armor notes come from the Ultima Codex's Armour values page (CC BY-SA 3.0). We reorganized the tables, merged the two, and reworded the notes. Where those disagree with the numbers in the official hintbook Balancing the Scales, as quoted on The Other Codex's Serpent Isle stats page, you'll see a gold "to verify" tag with both values.

How to read thisDamage is what the weapon does per hit; defense is what armor takes off. Add up defense across every piece you wear. Warmth is how well a piece keeps you warm: furs and leather help, and metal armor is negative. Items marked unique exist once in the game. We haven't listed weapon range, because our sources use different scales.
