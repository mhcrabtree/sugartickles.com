PotionsSerpent Isle

The eight familiar colors from Britannia, plus two new ones: a warming potion for the frozen north and a mana potion for your mage. Pothos the apothecary in Moonshade sells the everyday ones.

Sources

Pothos's prices come from Mike's RPG Center and Adrian Yau's Serpent Isle walkthrough, which also gives the price after haggling. The two new potions are described in Adrian Yau's guide and the Ultima Wiki. The tables and notes are our own. Where sources disagree you'll see a gold "to verify" tag.

How to read thisPrices are in guilders, Moonshade's money, per potion: what Pothos asks first, and roughly where haggling gets you. Three guilders make one monetari; see Treasure & Money to change other coins.

Cold and warmthUp north, the cold hurts your party unless they're dressed for it. The warming potion helps for a while, but furs, cloaks, and boots last. See the warmth column on Weapons & Armor.
