PotionsThe Black Gate

Potions in Britannia are known by their color. Here's what each of the eight colors does, who sells it and for how much, and the spots in the walkthrough where you'll want one.

Sources

Prices and sellers come from Mike's RPG Center, cross-checked against the Infinitron Ultima VII Potions list. Where the two disagree you'll see a gold "to verify" tag with both numbers; we show Mike's. The tables and notes are our own. Prices are before haggling, and shopkeepers will usually come down.

How to read thisPrices are in gold coins, per potion. The cheapest seller is in bold.

Kessler runs the apothecary on Noble Road, north of the market. Nicodemus sells from his house near Empath Abbey (you may need Unlock Magic to get in), Mariah from the Lycaeum, and Wis-Sur from his house on the gargoyle side of Vesper.

To verify while provingThe two lists disagree on most of Wis-Sur's prices in Vesper. The higher numbers may be his opening asks before haggling. Check them in our own game, and note the price you end up paying.
