Treasure & MoneyThe Black Gate

Shopkeepers only take gold coins. The gems, gold nuggets, and gold bars you pick up in dungeons are worth nothing until you cash them in, and in Britannia that means a trip to Britain.

Sources

Buyers and rates come from the Ultima Codex's Sellable items in Ultima VII (CC BY-SA 3.0), cross-checked against Gamer Corner's gem and gold nugget pages for where to find them. The tables and tips are our own.

Where to Cash In

Both buyers are in Britain, a short walk apart. Prices are in gold coins, per item.

ItemWho buys itWherePaysNotes
Gold barCynthiaThe Royal Mint100The best value for its weight, but heavy. Cash these in first
Gold nuggetCynthiaThe Royal Mint10One fan list says Sean pays 30; the Mint's 10 is what every other source gives
Gem (any kind)SeanThe jewelry shop30Same price whatever the gem. He sells them for 75, so don't buy any back

How to Sell

Neither buyer has a "sell" option on the first screen. You have to steer the conversation.

  1. Sean (gems): ask about his job, then about the materials he works with. He offers to buy gems.
  2. Cynthia (gold): talk with her about her work at the Mint, and she offers to trade nuggets and bars for coins.
  3. Come during shop hours. One source lists Sean's as 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. to verify
To verify while provingThe exact conversation keywords for Cynthia, and whether she or Sean needs the items in the Avatar's own pack. Note what works in our game.

Don't take what isn't yoursTreasure in shops and homes (Sean's gems under glass, the nugget at the Mint) is stealing, and guards come after you if anyone sees. Stick to dungeons and fallen enemies.
