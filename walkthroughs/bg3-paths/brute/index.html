Your Character

Build The Brute

A half-orc Berserker: Rage, a big weapon, and a deep distrust of anything that glows. This chapter picks the build.

Chapter 1

The Idea

Who the Brute is, and why a Barbarian

The Brute has no patience for wizards, wands or witchcraft. Problems get solved with an axe. A Barbarian is perfect: it rages, takes huge damage and dishes out more. The Berserker subclass goes all in on fury.

It's also one of the simplest classes in the game: there are no spells to prepare and few buttons to press. Great for a new player.

Patch 8 alternativePatch 8 added the Giant Barbarian, a hulking fighter that grows huge when it rages. It's another very Brute option. We built this page around the Berserker.

Guide

The Brute's Code

Our house rules for staying in character

RuleWhat it means
No castingYou never cast a spell, read a scroll or zap a wand. Magic is for people with something to prove.
Potions are fineA potion is just a drink. Drink it.
Shiny weapons are fineA sword that glows is still a sword.
No casters in the partyYour companions are warriors and rogues. No wizards, no clerics, no warlocks.
Rage firstIf it moves, you rage, then you hit it.
Break the doorIf a door is locked, you break it, shove it or hit it. No lockpicks.
Make it easierThe Code is about fun, not punishment. If a rule keeps you stuck, relax it. A shiny weapon you can't beat Act Two without is a good excuse.

Staying on Target

The Brute's Playthrough

Rage first. Ask questions never. Here's the plan.

Act One

Act One: Hit Things

Make friends with people who hit things

Your vows this act

  1. On the Nautiloid, get the Everburn Blade: make Zhalk drop it with a Command spell (cast by an ally), or fight him.
  2. Recruit Lae'zel, Astarion and Karlach. No casters.
  3. Break open locked doors and chests with Strength, or let Astarion pick them.
  4. Rage at the start of every fight, and let your friends rest between them.
  5. At the Grove, side with whoever hits hardest. You have no stake in the druids' arguments.
Recommended: your first big findTake the Skinburster from the Inquisitor's room in Crèche Y'llek at the end of Act One. With Rage, it makes you very hard to hurt.
To verify while provingConfirm who's present on the Nautiloid and how to get the Command spell used on Zhalk if no caster is in the party.

Step by step

Jump to: The Nautiloid · The Crash Site · The Emerald Grove · The Blighted Village & the Hag · The Risen Road & Karlach · The Goblin Camp · The Underdark & Grymforge · The Mountain Pass & the Crèche · Before You Leave Act One

Prologue: The Nautiloid

A mind flayer ship, on its way through Hell

  1. ⬆️ Level 1 (start): Rage, Unarmored Defense. Rage is your power button: it adds damage and resists weapon damage. Use it every fight.
  2. Wake in your pod and break out. Loot the raised walkways in the first room, and stay away from the larva pool in the middle: it bursts.
  3. Leave through the west door. In the next room, take the lift up to the elf with the exposed brain. Pass the check to see what it really is: an intellect devourer called Us.
  4. Decide what to do with Us: free it, leave it, or destroy it. If you free it, it tags along for a while.
  5. Step outside. A githyanki warrior, Lae'zel, drops in and joins you. Fight the three imps together.
  6. Enter the Helm. A cambion, Commander Zhalk, is fighting a mind flayer. You don't have to beat him. Your goal is the transponder on the far west side: reach it and connect its nerves when the mind flayer is distracted. Your path: You can also just hit Zhalk until he stops being a problem.
  7. Watch the ship break free of Hell and crash. You wake on the beach.
  8. 🎒 Get Everburn Blade. Commander Zhalk on the Nautiloid (prologue). You don't have to fight him: a Command spell makes him drop it. A big greatsword from the very first minutes. Command Zhalk to drop it. ⚠️ Missable

The Crash Site

The Ravaged Beach and the Roadside Cliffs · Quest: Find a Cure

  1. Look around the beach. Everyone from the ship has a tadpole, and nobody has turned yet. Your journal now tracks Find a Cure: the rest of Act One is following leads.
  2. Walk west along the beach. A pale elf runs up begging for help, then holds a knife to your throat. That's Astarion. Talk him down and he joins you.
  3. Follow the road north to the Roadside Cliffs. Two tiefling scouts, Nymessa and Damays, have Lae'zel in a cage. Talk them into letting her out, or shoot the cage, and recruit her.
  4. Explore the Dank Crypt near the beach. A sarcophagus holds a strange skeleton called Withers. Wake him: he moves into your camp and stays useful all game. He can re-pick your class and abilities, and bring back dead companions.
  5. Make camp and take a long rest. Your companions talk about the tadpole. Talk to each one before you move on.
  6. ⬆️ Level 2 (around now): Reckless Attack, Danger Sense. Attack with advantage, at the price of taking hits. Fine for a Brute.

The Emerald Grove

North of the Roadside Cliffs · Quests: Save the Refugees, Save Arabella

  1. At the Grove gate, goblins are attacking tieflings. Join the fight on the tieflings' side. A warlock named Wyll fights beside you.
  2. Inside, talk Zevlor (the tieflings' leader) and Aradin (an adventurer who lost his druid guide) out of their argument. Zevlor explains the goblin threat and the Grove's troubles.
  3. Go to the Inner Sanctum. A tiefling girl, Arabella, has been caught stealing the druids' Sacred Idol, and Kagha is about to punish her. Talk Kagha into letting her go (Save Arabella).
  4. Never take the Sacred Idol, whoever asks you to. Taking it makes the druids finish the ritual and throws the tieflings out.
  5. Kagha has begun the Rite of Thorns, which will seal the Grove. Their leader, Halsin, is missing: the goblins took him.
  6. Talk to the healer Nettie. She can't cure your tadpole, but she points you toward Halsin. Be honest with her and accept her terms, or she'll try to poison you.
  7. Meet the bard Alfira and the gnome author Volo. Both have side quests, and traders like Arron sell useful gear.
  8. ⬆️ Level 3 (around now): Berserker: Frenzy. Frenzy gives you an extra attack while raging. It's what makes the Berserker a Berserker.

The Blighted Village & the Hag

The Blighted Village, the Riverside Teahouse and the Sunlit Wetlands · Quests: Rescue the Gnome, Save Mayrina

  1. Head west to the Blighted Village. Goblins hold it. Talk your way past the gate or fight.
  2. A gnome, Barcus Wroot, is tied to a windmill. Free him (Rescue the Gnome), then search the village, including the cellar under the old apothecary.
  3. The cave to the north belongs to an owlbear and her cub. It's a hard fight at low level: come back later.
  4. South of the village, two brothers are arguing with an old woman about their sister. She's Auntie Ethel, and the sister is Mayrina. This starts Save Mayrina.
  5. Follow Ethel to her Riverside Teahouse in the swamp. She's a hag, and she'll offer to remove your tadpole in return for an eye. Don't: she can't do it.
  6. Follow her through the fireplace into her lair. Fight past the masked servants, find Mayrina in her cage, and put out the fire beneath it quickly or she dies.
  7. When Ethel is nearly beaten she offers a bargain. Free Mayrina: it's the kind choice.
  8. ⬆️ Level 4 (around now): Ability Improvement or feat. +2 Strength, or the Tavern Brawler or Great Weapon Master feat.

The Risen Road & Karlach

East of the Grove · Quests: Hunt the Devil, Rescue the Grand Duke

  1. At the Toll House on the road, a group of paladins led by Anders is hunting a devil and wants your help.
  2. Go down the cliff and across the stream. The 'devil' is Karlach, a tiefling who escaped from Hell. Hear her side, then decide whom to believe (Hunt the Devil).
  3. Side with Karlach, and she joins you: one of the best fighters in the game, and one of the warmest.
  4. Further along, the inn at Waukeen's Rest is on fire with people trapped inside, including Councillor Florrick (Rescue the Grand Duke). Put out the flames and get everyone out. Do this before you long rest or fast travel, or they die.

The Goblin Camp

Northwest of the Blighted Village · Quests: Save the First Druid, Defeat the Goblins

  1. Walk in through the front gate. The goblins think your tadpole makes you a 'True Soul', a chosen servant of their god, the Absolute. Use that.
  2. Inside the Shattered Sanctum three leaders rule the camp: Priestess Gut, Dror Ragzlin and the drow Minthara. Each wants to know where the Grove is.
  3. Find Halsin in bear form in the worg pens behind the Sanctum. Help him fight the goblins. He asks you to deal with the leaders so the Grove is safe.
  4. In a room off the Sanctum, goblins are torturing Liam, one of Aradin's group. Talk them off him and free him. He tells you about the Nightsong.
  5. Defeat all three leaders. If you can, knock Minthara out (non-lethal attacks) rather than killing her. That keeps the door open to freeing her in Act Two.
  6. Return to Halsin, then go back to the Grove. The Rite of Thorns ends, the tieflings prepare to leave, and they throw a party at camp.
  7. ⬆️ Level 5 (around now): Extra Attack, Fast Movement. You hit twice and run faster.

The Underdark & Grymforge

Below the Defiled Temple, the Zhentarim Hideout or the Whispering Depths · Quests: Defeat the Duergar Intruders, Save the Grymforge Gnomes

  1. The easiest way down is the Defiled Temple under the Shattered Sanctum. Solve the Selûnite door puzzle (a dwarf named Brian's poem holds the clues) and keep going down.
  2. In the Myconid Colony, the mushroom folk ask you to deal with duergar intruders. A myconid named Glut has plans of his own.
  3. Visit Omeluum, a friendly mind flayer in the colony. He'll study your tadpole.
  4. Find the old Selûnite Outpost. A book there, In Search of the Nightsong, says the Nightsong is held in a Sharran fortress near Moonrise Towers.
  5. Take a skiff across the lake to Grymforge. Duergar have enslaved deep gnomes there. Free them, whether or not you free the True Soul Nere trapped behind a rockfall.
  6. Optional: below Grymforge lies the Adamantine Forge, where you can craft some of Act One's best gear if you defeat its guardian, Grym.
  7. The elevator in Grymforge goes up to the Shadow-Cursed Lands. That's your way into Act Two on this route.
  8. 🎒 Get Adamantine Scale or Splint Mail. Crafted at the Adamantine Forge in Grymforge. Heavy armor is optional but very sturdy. 🔨 Craft it

The Mountain Pass & the Crèche

The Risen Road, the Mountain Pass and the Rosymorn Monastery · Quest: Find the Githyanki Crèche

  1. With Lae'zel in the party, cross west along the Risen Road. A githyanki patrol is arguing with some Flaming Fists, and a red dragon drops from the sky. Lae'zel runs to meet its rider, Kith'rak Voss. Go with her.
  2. At the Rosymorn Monastery Trail, summon the cable car with the wheel and ride across. Climb into the monastery through the broken windows.
  3. Optional: the monastery hides the Blood of Lathander, a legendary mace, behind a puzzle (Find the Blood of Lathander).
  4. Below the monastery is the githyanki crèche, Crèche Y'llek. The gith distrust outsiders, so talk carefully (and let Lae'zel do the talking if she's with you).
  5. In the infirmary, Inquisitor W'wargaz offers to cure you in the Zaith'isk, a githyanki machine. It doesn't go to plan.
  6. The Mountain Pass leads down into the Shadow-Cursed Lands: your other way into Act Two.
  7. 🎒 Get The Skinburster. A chest in the Inquisitor's room, Crèche Y'llek. A halberd that makes Rage even tougher. 📦 Chest

Before You Leave Act One

A checklist for your last day in the Wilderness

  1. Make sure the tieflings are safe and you've celebrated at camp.
  2. Check that Florrick is out of Waukeen's Rest, and that Mayrina is free (or paid for).
  3. Visit both the Underdark and the crèche, or decide which to skip.
  4. Talk to every companion at camp and finish their camp scenes. Some only trigger in Act One.
  5. Sell your junk and buy what you can afford from traders like Arron and Dammon at the Grove.
  6. Make a manual save before you descend. Act Two takes you somewhere you can't easily come back from.
  7. ⬆️ Level 6 (around now): Mindless Rage. You can't be charmed or frightened while you rage.

Act Two

Act Two: Hit Harder

The shadows can't hurt what you can't see

Your vows this act

  1. Look for the Mighty Cloth and the Enraging Heart Garb from the Last Light and Moonrise traders.
  2. Use Reckless Attack on every boss.
  3. Keep your potions stocked: no healer means no safety net.
To verify while provingReplace with the exact Act Two quests after playing.

Step by step

Jump to: Into the Shadows · Last Light Inn · Reithwin Town · Moonrise Towers · The Gauntlet of Shar · Defeat Ketheric Thorm

Into the Shadows

The Ruined Battlefield · Quests: Seek Protection from the Shadow Curse, Follow the Convoy

  1. Step out of the dark. The curse hurts anyone not standing in light. Take a torch from the braziers near the exit and keep it lit.
  2. If you came from the Underdark: a group of Harpers is fighting shadows nearby. Help them. Their leader, Lassandra, sends you to the last safe place: the Last Light Inn.
  3. If you came from the Mountain Pass: a goblin mistakes you for a True Soul and tells you to wait for a guide, the drider Kar'niss, whose moonlantern keeps the curse off. Harpers ambush the convoy on the way.
  4. Either way, head for the Last Light Inn.

Last Light Inn

Quests: Resolve the Abduction, Lift the Shadow Curse, Rescue the Tieflings

  1. At the gate, the veteran Harper Jaheira tests you with a tadpole specimen and suspects you're a True Soul. Someone from the Grove can vouch for you, or show her your Mysterious Artefact.
  2. Jaheira explains that the cult's leader at Moonrise Towers is Ketheric Thorm, and he can't die. The Harpers want to stop him.
  3. Go upstairs to meet Isobel, the Selûnite cleric whose magic shields the inn. Talking to her triggers an attack: a winged man named Marcus tries to carry her off.
  4. Protect Isobel. If she's taken or killed, the shield falls and everyone at the inn is lost to the curse.
  5. The tieflings from the Grove are here, but some are missing: they were taken to Moonrise Towers (Rescue the Tieflings).
  6. Shop at the inn: the quartermaster, Talli, sells some of Act Two's best gear.
  7. 🎒 Get The Mighty Cloth. Quartermaster Talli, Last Light Inn. A cloak that raises Strength, perfect for an unarmored Barbarian. 🛒 Trader

Reithwin Town

West of the inn, and the House of Healing

  1. Reithwin was a prosperous Selûnite town before the curse. Explore it by day, torches lit.
  2. Members of the Thorm family still haunt their posts: Thisobald at the tollhouse, Gerringothe at the Waning Moon bar, Malus at the House of Healing.
  3. In the town and nearby, the House in Deep Shadow holds secrets and a quest from Olivar (Hide and Seek).
  4. Hold off on the Grand Mausoleum, north of town, until you've been to Moonrise Towers.
  5. 🥚 Easter egg: Rat Bat. The Waning Moon pub in the Shadow-Cursed Lands.

Moonrise Towers

Across the bridge from Reithwin · Quests: Infiltrate Moonrise Towers, Rescue Wulbren, Rescue the Tieflings

  1. Walk in as a True Soul. In the audience chamber, Ketheric's lieutenant Z'rell is questioning the goblins about what happened at the camp.
  2. If you knocked Minthara out in Act One, she's here too, begging for her life. Ketheric condemns her.
  3. Talk to Z'rell behind the throne. Prove your faith and she sends you after Balthazar, Ketheric's necromancer, last seen at the Thorm Mausoleum. She gives you a key to his room.
  4. Search Balthazar's room for its hidden area. His notes reveal what the 'Nightsong' really is.
  5. Find the prison. In the cells are the gnome Wulbren, captured tieflings (including Cal and Lia) and others. Break them out and lead them to the boat or out on foot.
  6. Once everyone is free, return to the Last Light Inn to collect your thanks.
  7. Moonrise Towers' traders (Lann Tarv, Roah Moonglow and Araj Oblodra) sell strong gear. Check all three.
  8. 🎒 Get Enraging Heart Garb. Lann Tarv, Moonrise Towers. A garb that raises Constitution and rewards raging. 🛒 Trader
  9. 🎒 Get Halberd of Vigilance. Lann Tarv, Moonrise Towers. Reactions that hit harder. 🛒 Trader
  10. 🎒 Get Titanstring Bow. Lann Tarv, Moonrise Towers. A bow for the one time you need to hit something far away. 🛒 Trader
  11. ⬆️ Level 7 (around now): Feral Instinct. Better initiative, and you can rage when surprised.

The Gauntlet of Shar

Through the Grand Mausoleum north of Reithwin · Quests: Find Ketheric Thorm's Relic, Find the Nightsong

  1. Solve the mausoleum's puzzle and descend into the Gauntlet of Shar, an ancient temple to the goddess of loss. Snuff out every light in the entrance hall to open the way.
  2. Balthazar is already here and orders you to clear his way. Help him or fight him: either way you need what lies below.
  3. Collect four Umbral Gems. Three come from the Trials of Shar: the Soft-Step Trial (sneak through a maze), the Self-Same Trial (fight shadow copies of your party) and the Faith-Leap Trial (cross an invisible path).
  4. The fourth gem belongs to Yurgir, an orthon in the east wing, who has a contract with the devil Raphael he'd love to break.
  5. Look for hidden chests in the Gauntlet's side rooms. Several of Act Two's best items are tucked away.
  6. Before you open the way into the Shadowfell, finish what you want to finish in Act One's lands and at Moonrise Towers. The Shadowfell is a point of no return.
  7. Use the gems to open the Shadowfell, and find the Nightsong.
  8. ⬆️ Level 8 (around now): Ability Improvement. More Strength.

Defeat Ketheric Thorm

Moonrise Towers and the Mind Flayer Colony beneath it · Quest: Defeat Ketheric Thorm

  1. Back at the Last Light Inn, Jaheira gathers the Harpers and Flaming Fists to storm Moonrise Towers. Join the assault.
  2. Fight your way up to the roof. Ketheric waits there, and now he can be hurt. Win the fight.
  3. When he falls the way opens into the Mind Flayer Colony below. Follow him down.
  4. Explore the colony. It's where the tadpoles are made, and where you learn what the cult has been building.
  5. Face Ketheric one last time.
  6. After the fight the story takes a turn. Act Two ends at your new camp, Wyrm's Lookout, on the road to Baldur's Gate.
  7. ⬆️ Level 9 (around now): Brutal Critical. Critical hits get bigger.

Act Three

Act Three: Hit Everything

The final brawl

Step by step

Jump to: Rivington · Wyrm's Rock & Gortash · The Lower City · Orin & the Temple of Bhaal · Confront the Elder Brain

Rivington

The outer district south of the river · Quests: Solve the Open Hand Temple Murders, Find the Missing Letters

  1. From Wyrm's Lookout, walk west into Rivington. Refugees fill the streets: the city won't let them in.
  2. Look around the Circus of the Last Days. It's great fun, and a few of the acts aren't what they seem.
  3. At the Open Hand Temple, someone has murdered the priests. Investigate (Solve the Open Hand Temple Murders). The trail leads to Orin.
  4. At Sword Coast Couriers, letters for your companions have gone missing (Find the Missing Letters). Track them down: each one adds to a companion's story.
  5. Find the camp of the Gur, the monster hunters. They're after one of your companions.
  6. Cross Wyrm's Crossing, the bridge into the city.
  7. 🎒 Get Bonespike Garb. Exxvikyap, the Rivington blacksmith. Temporary HP for the Brute. 🛒 Trader
  8. 🎒 Get Sethan. Ferg Drogher, Rivington refugees. A huge axe. 🛒 Trader
  9. 🥚 Easter egg: Sethan, the JoJo greataxe. Ferg Drogher, among the Rivington refugees.
  10. ⬆️ Level 10 (around now): Subclass feature. More fury.

Wyrm's Rock & Gortash

Wyrm's Rock Fortress, the Iron Throne and the Steel Watch Foundry · Quests: Get Gortash's Netherstone, Rescue the Grand Duke

  1. Enver Gortash is being crowned Archduke of Baldur's Gate in Wyrm's Rock Fortress. Go up to the ceremony.
  2. Gortash makes you an offer: kill Orin and rule the city with him. You don't have to answer yet.
  3. He's guarded by the Steel Watch, giant clockwork soldiers. Before you take him on, go to the Steel Watch Foundry in the Lower City and shut it down (Disable the Steel Watch).
  4. Find the Iron Throne, an underwater prison off the coast. Duke Ravengard, Wyll's father, is held there with a group of gnomes. Get them out before the prison floods.
  5. Then return to Wyrm's Rock and take Gortash's Netherstone.
  6. 🎒 Get Horns of the Berserker. Entharl Danthelon, Wyrmgate bridge. +2 to attack when you're wounded. 🛒 Trader
  7. 🎒 Get Balduran's Giantslayer. The secret base under Wyrmgate Prison, or Wyll's storyline. A legendary greatsword. 👀 Easy to miss

The Lower City

The Lower City and its sewers

  1. Make the Elfsong Tavern your first stop. You'll hear about half the city's trouble there.
  2. At Sorcerous Sundries, the wizard Lorroakan is still offering a reward for the Nightsong. If you freed Dame Aylin, think carefully about what you tell him.
  3. Astarion: his old master Cazador lives in a palace in the Lower City. Visit it when Astarion is ready.
  4. Karlach: find Dammon, the tiefling blacksmith from the Grove, and hear what he can do about her infernal engine.
  5. Lae'zel: finish her business with the githyanki queen, Vlaakith, and decide what to do about Orpheus.
  6. Explore. The Lower City is crowded with side quests: a guild war, a printing press, a haunted bookshop, a devil's house, and more. Buy and loot as you go.
  7. 🎒 Get Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength. The House of Hope archive. Sets your Strength to 23. 📜 Quest reward
  8. 🎒 Get Amulet of Greater Health. House of Hope. Raises Constitution to 23. 📜 Quest reward
  9. 🎒 Get Bonespike Helmet. The Voiceless Penitent, in the sewers. An area-damage helmet while you rage. 💀 Boss drop
  10. 🥚 Easter egg: "Boo is not used to this taking of turns". A Minsc combat line.
  11. 🥚 Easter egg: Minsc's room. The Lower City Sewers, in the room where you recruit him.
  12. 🥚 Easter egg: "Rudolph Van Richtover My Dead Body" ⚠️. Defeating Cazador Szarr.
  13. ⬆️ Level 11 (around now): Relentless Rage. A rage that keeps you on your feet.

Orin & the Temple of Bhaal

Candulhallow's Tombstones, the Murder Tribunal and the Temple of Bhaal under the city · Quest: Get Orin's Netherstone

  1. Once you're in the Lower City, Orin the Red kidnaps a companion who's waiting at camp. She wants you to kill Gortash for her.
  2. Find your way into her temple. Investigate the city's serial murders (Investigate the Murders) or kill Gortash first: either way you get the password to the Murder Tribunal, hidden under Candulhallow's Tombstones.
  3. At the Tribunal you meet Sarevok Anchev. Win his trust or fight him for the Amulet of Bhaal and the map to the temple.
  4. The Temple of Bhaal is in the Undercity Ruins. Hold the amulet to the temple door, fight past the assassins, and face Orin.
  5. Take Orin's Netherstone from her dagger, and rescue your companion.
  6. 🎒 Get Sword of Chaos. Sarevok, near the Murder Tribunal. Unconfirmed. A huge greatsword. 📜 Quest reward
  7. 🥚 Easter egg: Sarevok's gear ⚠️. Looted from Sarevok's corpse.
  8. ⬆️ Level 12 (around now): Final Ability Improvement. You're done. Max level.

Confront the Elder Brain

The Morphic Pool and the Upper City · Quests: Gather Your Allies, Confront the Elder Brain

  1. Open the Gather Your Allies quest and check who you've rallied: the tieflings, the druids, the gnomes, the Harpers, the githyanki and more. The longer the list, the easier the finale.
  2. With all three Netherstones in hand, head for the Morphic Pool, where the elder brain feeds.
  3. From here the game moves to its finale in the Upper City. Finish everything else first. There's no coming back for side quests.
  4. Make your last choices, and see your story through.
