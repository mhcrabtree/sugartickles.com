Easter Egg GuideNods, jokes and secrets

Baldur's Gate 3 is full of winks: to the earlier games, to Larian's Divinity series, and to films, shows and books. This is a guide to the ones worth hunting, with where they are, what you need to see them, and which of our paths is most likely to bump into each.

Draft statusThis list comes mostly from the bg3.wiki pages on cultural references and on references to earlier Baldur's Gate games, reworded. The wiki rarely gives the act, so wherever we've guessed we say (our guess). We'll confirm each entry in our own playthrough, and add our own finds.
How the paths use thisEach path has an Easter Eggs on Your Path chapter, and each act chapter lists the ones nearby. Entries marked ⚠️ are spoilers: the details are hidden until you open them.

Act One

The wilderness and the Underdark

Easter eggWhereWhat it isWhat you needPaths that meet it
Anders and the Sword of JusticeThe Risen Road. A paladin named Anders leads the group in the Toll House.A nod to Anders and Justice from Dragon Age.Just meet him. The Paragon visits him anyway.Paragon
Faldorn's CanticleA book in Kagha's chest at the Emerald Grove.Written by Faldorn, the druid companion from the original Baldur's Gate.Loot Kagha's chest (the Paragon investigates Kagha).Paragon, Druid
"She Cannot Be Caged!"The achievement for saving Sazza in the Goblin Camp.Probably an echo of a famous line from Baldur's Gate 2.Free Sazza. The Absolute brings her to Minthara anyway.Absolute
Acolytes of the Demonweb PitsGrymforge, in the Underdark: the Lur spiders.Turning the spiders toward Lolth fulfils the Acolyte background goal, a Forgotten Realms nod.You need the Acolyte background and the right dialogue outcome.Cleric

Rat BatThe Waning Moon pub in the Shadow-Cursed Lands.A spiky greatclub that may nod to Charlie's Rat Stick in It's Always Sunny.Loot it.Brute, Tipsy Fist
Alaundo's prophecyJaheira recites it before the assault on Moonrise Towers. (act is our guess)
⚠️ Spoiler: show

A sage's prophecy from the earlier games, which seems to point at Dame Aylin.

Have Jaheira with you. The trigger isn't stated.Druid
Balthazar's borrowed bonesAsk the necromancer Balthazar about his name. (act is our guess)
⚠️ Spoiler: show

He admits he took some bones from the Balthazar of Baldur's Gate 2.

Talk to him.Scholar
"You must gather your party"The entrance to the Shadowfell. (act is our guess)
⚠️ Spoiler: show

The Narrator says it if the full party isn't nearby, just like map exits in the older games.

Walk in without your companions. Try it solo.Lone Wanderer

The Warlock's threatWyrm's Rock Fortress, in a conversation with Elin. (act is our guess)A Warlock can threaten to write her name in the Book of Vile Darkness. It's a very hard Deception check (DC 30), and a failure gets a rude reply.Be a Warlock.Lone Wanderer
Marvin the talking skullThe Wyrm's Rock prison. (act is our guess)A talking skull that can help you escape. It resembles Morte from Planescape: Torment and Murray from Monkey Island.Interact with him.Lone Wanderer
Minsc's roomThe Lower City Sewers, in the room where you recruit him.It's full of nods to the old party: wooden wings for Aerie, a cracked mask for Dynaheir, a turnip for Jan Jansen, a mailed fist for Keldorn and a tiny warhammer for Mazzy.Reach the room where Minsc can be recruited.Brute, Showman, Tipsy Fist, Ranger, Fighter

Gorion's mausoleumThe Lower City.A mausoleum for "Lord Gregorion Gorion", a probable wink at Gorion from the first game. The wiki isn't sure.Find it.—
Roveer's StorehouseA quest in the Lower City.You clear rats for Roveer, echoing Reevor's Storehouse in the first Baldur's Gate.Take the quest.Paragon, Fighter
Naaber in Carm's GarmsThe Lower City.A very talkative wannabe adventurer, in the line of Noober, Neeber and Nüber from the older games. Speak with Dead reveals his family ties, and finishing his dialogue earns a small reward.Talk to him. Speak with Dead helps (Bards and Clerics can cast it).Showman
The Wyrmway chess puzzleThe Chamber of Strategy, in the Wyrmway. (act is our guess)A real chess puzzle.Visit the chamber.Scholar
Sethan, the JoJo greataxeFerg Drogher, among the Rivington refugees.The axe and its abilities nod to Alessi from JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, who de-ages people.Buy or take the axe.Brute, Fighter
