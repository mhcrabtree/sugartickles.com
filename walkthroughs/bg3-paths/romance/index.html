Beyond the CompanionsSix one-time encounters, and what they cost

Not every romance in Baldur's Gate 3 is a companion. Six other characters offer one-time encounters, mostly in Act Three. Some are harmless fun, one can end a romance you've worked on, and one can end your run. This guide says which, and which of our paths each one fits.

The Six Which Paths Fit Who It Hurts
Mature content and spoilersThis guide covers adult encounters at a high level, and the characters' roles in the plot are spoilers, so they're hidden until you open them. All of these appear in Act Three, except where noted.
Draft statusCompiled and reworded from bg3.wiki's Romance page and each character's page. We read most but not all of each page, so the details marked below are the ones to confirm in play.
The big pictureThe wiki describes these as separate trysts with no romance progression, not relationships. None needs a particular gender or body type. Only Mizora has a stated break-up risk, and Haarlep carries a game-over risk if you surrender completely.

The Six

Where, how, and what it costs

WhoWhere and whenHow it startsRisks and consequences
Haarlep
⚠️ Who

An incubus who serves the devil Raphael.

Act Three. Raphael's boudoir, House of Hope.Talk to them, and be honest: they see through lies and attack. They ask for a party member to 'play' with them in return for information.Resist with a DC 18 Wisdom or Performance check, or a DC 25 Constitution check, or fight. Surrendering 'body but not mind' gets the key to Raphael's safe. Surrendering body and soul is a game over (or a companion's permanent death). Killing Haarlep drops the Helldusk Gloves, which you lose if you don't. Several companions take a -1 approval hit for the 'body but not mind' route, and most approve of fighting.
Mizora
⚠️ Who

Wyll's fiendish patron.

Act Three, at camp. Needs Wyll in the story, and Mizora to have survived the Mind Flayer Colony.Speak with her near the Audience Chamber stairs at Wyrm's Rock or at the Lower City transition. Rest until her camp scene, accept when the exclamation mark appears, and rest again before the Iron Throne, which overwrites her offer.The risky one. One night only. It always ends Gale's, Lae'zel's, Minthara's and Wyll's romances. It doesn't end Halsin's or Shadowheart's (or Astarion's, if he's a Vampire Ascendant), and Astarion as spawn and Karlach depend on your dialogue. Wyll's approval drops by 5, and more if he's with you. Declining closes the path for good.
Naoise
⚠️ Who

A Wood Elf courtesan with a surprising client.

Act Three. Sharess' Caress, Wyrm's Crossing: her room, Nymph's Grotto.Enter her room, which starts a scene that ends in a fight. Afterward a passive DC 10 Insight check unlocks extra options, including a Wizard line and a Warlock line.A temporary 'rapture' buff and background inspirations (Guild Artisan, Haunted One, Entertainer). No stated effect on companion romances. If you fail the check or decline, you get a Superior Elixir of Arcane Cultivation (unconfirmed).
Nym OrlithAct Three. Sharess' Caress, in their private room.Pay 500 gold for one twin or 1000 for both, or finish the quest Find the 'Stern Librarian' Ffion and ask Amira for a reward. Everyone else must leave the room first.Voice-only scenes. Guild Artisan and Entertainer inspirations. No consequences listed for your romances. You can back out and get the fee back. Repeatable by paying again.
Sorn OrlithAct Three. Sharess' Caress, in their private room.Same as Nym. Sorn has several comic options.Same rewards as Nym. Companions who'd join a group scene: Halsin always; Gale with a DC 25 Persuasion check (he sends a projection); Astarion only after The Pale Elf quest; Shadowheart not until her final romance scene; Karlach refuses a foursome but accepts one twin. The rest refuse.
The Emperor
⚠️ Who

The mind flayer who has guarded your dreams. Heavy spoilers: it's the lost founder of Baldur's Gate.

Act Three, on a long rest after you complete Visit the Emperor's Old Hideout (the Elfsong Tavern).Treat the Emperor like a person, with empathy and no hostility to illithids. You can choose its Dream Guardian form or its native form. Either counts.Rejecting it ('Absolutely not, you freak!') or killing the Dream Guardian in Act One locks it out for good, and destroying your tadpole offends it. The primary scene doesn't harm other romances (the wiki's note on Lae'zel was cut off), and it unlocks the 'Mind Blown' achievement.

Who It Hurts

The consequences for your companion romances

EncounterEnds or damages
MizoraEnds Gale, Lae'zel, Minthara and Wyll. Spares Halsin and Shadowheart. Depends on dialogue: Karlach, and Astarion as a spawn.
HaarlepNo romance ending is stated, but 'body but not mind' costs a small approval hit from Astarion, Lae'zel, Gale, Wyll, Karlach, Jaheira, Minthara and Minsc.
Nym and SornNone listed. Minthara disapproves of sleeping with them and refuses group scenes.
