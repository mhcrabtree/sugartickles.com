A to Z

54 terms, in plain English

TermWhat it means
Ability scoreOne of your six core numbers (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma). See Dice & Numbers.
Absolute, theA mysterious power the goblins and cultists worship. A plot element: play to learn more.
ActOne of the game's three big chapters.
ActionThe main thing you do on a turn: attack, cast a spell, dash, and so on.
AdvantageRoll two d20s and keep the higher.
AC (Armor Class)How hard you are to hit. Enemies must roll this high or higher.
ApprovalHow much a companion likes you.
BackgroundWhat your hero did before the game. Gives two skills and an Inspiration goal.
Bonus actionA smaller action you can take along with your main one.
CampYour home base. You rest, store things and talk to companions here.
Camp suppliesFood and drink that pay for long rests.
CantripA free spell you can cast as often as you like. Your everyday attack or trick. See What's a Cantrip?
Check (ability or skill check)A d20 roll to see if you succeed at something uncertain, like persuading someone.
ClassYour hero's profession: Fighter, Wizard, Rogue, and so on.
CompanionA character who travels with you.
ConcentrationHolding one lasting spell at a time.
ConditionA status effect, like prone, poisoned or frightened.
Critical hitA natural 20 on an attack: it always hits and doubles the damage dice.
d20A twenty-sided die.
DC (Difficulty Class)The target number a check or saving throw must meet.
DisadvantageRoll two d20s and keep the lower.
DM (Dungeon Master)The person who runs a tabletop game. In this game, the computer is the DM.
DownedAt 0 HP, rolling death saves.
ExpertiseDouble proficiency bonus on a skill.
FeatA special ability you can pick instead of raising your abilities at certain levels.
Fighting styleA small combat bonus Fighters, Paladins and Rangers choose.
Hit points (HP)How much damage you can take before you're downed.
Illithid (mind flayer)A tentacle-faced monster. The source of your tadpole.
InitiativeA roll at the start of a fight that decides turn order.
InspirationA re-roll point you earn for acting in character with your background.
Long restA full night's rest at camp that restores everything.
ModifierThe bonus or penalty an ability score gives to rolls.
MulticlassAdding levels in a second class.
NPCA non-player character: anyone who isn't you or a companion.
Opportunity attackA free attack on an enemy who moves away from you.
Origin characterOne of the six pre-written companions who can also be your hero.
PartyThe group that travels with you: up to four.
ProficiencyBeing trained at something. You add your proficiency bonus to it.
ProneKnocked down.
ReactionA response on someone else's turn.
RespecRebuilding a character's class and abilities, via Withers.
Rest (short or long)Recovering HP and abilities.
Saving throw (save)A roll to resist something that's happening to you.
Short restA quick rest anywhere. You get two between long rests.
SkillA specific talent, like Stealth or Persuasion. There are 18.
Spell slotA limited resource that powers leveled spells.
SubclassA specialty within your class, like Oath of Devotion for a Paladin. Some classes pick it at level 1, others at 2 or 3. See Subclasses.
TadpoleThe mind flayer parasite in your head.
TavFans' nickname for your custom hero.
Traveller's ChestShared storage at every camp.
True SoulA cultist of the Absolute who carries a tadpole. The goblins think you are one.
Turn-based modeThe mode where everyone takes turns, used for fights.
WithersThe skeleton at your camp who respecs, revives and hires.
XPExperience points. They make you level up.
