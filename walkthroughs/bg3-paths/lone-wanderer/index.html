Build The Lone Wanderer

A Hexblade Warlock: a blade-wielding Charisma fighter with short-rest magic. Strong on its own, and simple to build. This chapter picks the build.

Chapter 1

The Idea

Who the Lone Wanderer is, and why a Hexblade

The Lone Wanderer turns every companion down. No Astarion, no Shadowheart, no Gale: just you. Is it possible? Yes. Players have finished the game solo, and a small fraction of players reached the end with no companions at all. It's a challenge run, though: there's no "lone wolf" bonus, and you won't earn extra experience for going it alone.

For a solo build we picked the Hexblade Warlock, a Patch 8 subclass. It wears medium armor, carries a shield, fights with Charisma, and its spell slots come back on a short rest. It needs only one main stat, and one tier list rates it among the best subclasses in the game.

Honest caveatsWe could confirm the basics, but not the details. We don't yet know every scene that forces a companion into your party, or exactly which quests change without companions. Treat this page as a plan to test, and expect us to learn as we play.

Solo Rules

How to play alone, and what to expect

RuleWhat it means
Recruit no oneDon't ask any companion to join. If one joins you for a scene, send them to camp afterward.
No hirelingsHirelings are companions by another name. Skip them.
Temporary helpers don't countSome characters join you for a quest and leave on their own. They don't take a party slot, so we allow them.
No romanceThat's the cost of loneliness.
DifficultyWe'd start on Explorer, then work up. There's no solo bonus, so a hard setting makes a hard run harder.
The solo toolkitPlayers who've done this recommend: a high armor class (around 22 is reachable), plenty of healing potions, scrolls for spells you lack, and a way to avoid fights altogether. Misty Step, invisibility, and Pass Without Trace all help.

Staying on Target

The Lone Wanderer's Playthrough

One character, one blade, no net. Here's the plan.

Act One

Act One: Walk Alone

Refuse every offer

  1. On the Nautiloid, don't seek out companions. Free yourself and escape.
  2. On the beach, don't add Astarion, Gale, Shadowheart or Lae'zel to your party.
  3. Pick your fights. Use Eldritch Blast from range and Armor of Agathys up close.
  4. Buy healing potions and keep a stock at all times.
  5. Rest often: your spell slots return on a short rest.
Recommended: your first big findGet the Amulet of Misty Step from the chest in Priestess Gut's chambers, guarded by the ogre Polma. It's your escape button.
To verify while provingConfirm which companions approach you on their own, and how to turn them down.
Act Two

Act Two: Alone in the Dark

The shadows don't care how many you are

  1. Grab the Ring of Shadow and the Shifting Corpus Ring for stealth and escape.
  2. Avoid fights that don't pay off.
  3. Keep your potions stocked, and your short rests regular.

Act Three

Act Three: The Last Stand

The city, alone

  1. Seek out the Helm of Balduran in the secret base under Wyrmgate Prison.
  2. Keep your escape tools and your potions, and take the final fights one at a time.
  3. At the finale, face the end alone.
