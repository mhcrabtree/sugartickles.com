Your Character

Build The Absolute

A dark Shadow Magic Sorcerer: a caster who takes power rather than earns it. This chapter tells you everything except how they look.

Chapter 1

The Idea

Who the Absolute is, and why a Sorcerer

The Absolute doesn't resist the tadpole. They take its power, and everything else that's on offer. A Sorcerer casts through raw talent rather than study, which suits someone who takes power rather than earns it. Patch 8 added the Shadow Magic Sorcerer, chosen at level 1: dark, patient and fitting.

The partner is Minthara, a drow commander who respects strength and nothing else. This path is a real commitment: the raid on the Emerald Grove changes Act One for good.

Know the cost before you startSiding with Minthara means raiding the Emerald Grove. The tieflings and druids die, Halsin is lost, Karlach and Wyll leave your party for good (or can't be recruited), and Gale leaves unless you pass an Insight or Deception check. The Absolute accepts that. If you don't, pick another path.

Staying on Target

The Absolute's Playthrough

Take the power. Choose the dark side, again and again. Here's how to stay on it.

Act One

Act One: Embrace the Worm

The choices that open the dark road

  1. Use the tadpole's powers when the game offers them, and never refuse them.
  2. Reach the Goblin Camp's Shattered Sanctum and meet Minthara. She recognizes you as one of the Absolute's chosen.
  3. Tell her where the Emerald Grove is: talk to her, or bring her Sazza (the Save the Goblin Sazza quest).
  4. Take a long rest. The raid starts after your next rest.
  5. When she orders it, open the Grove's gate for the goblins and join the slaughter.
  6. Accept the Absolute's reward at the goblins' celebration. Minthara offers a night with you there; it's your choice.
  7. Take the Minthara's Spider's Lyre and follow her instructions to Moonrise Towers.
What the raid costsThe tieflings and druids die; Halsin is lost; Karlach and Wyll leave; Gale leaves unless you pass an Insight or Deception check. Dammon the smith dies, which means fewer rare traders later and Karlach's engine can't be fixed. Lifting the Shadow Curse in Act Two becomes impossible.
If you change your mindYou can start the raid and then side with the tieflings, but the whole Goblin Camp turns on you. If you'd rather keep your companions, choose a different path.

Act Two

Act Two: Rise

Free her, and keep her

  1. Follow Minthara to Moonrise Towers: she's on trial there, and Ketheric condemns her.
  2. Get to the prison where two gnome inquisitors torture her. Free her by killing them or talking.
  3. Escort her out by stealth, dialogue or violence. Do not help erase her mind.
  4. Invite her to your camp. That recruits her.
  5. Lean into your tadpole powers, and choose the harshest option where the game offers one.
How to lose her for goodLetting her die in Act One, helping erase her mind in the prison, or refusing her offer twice in Act Two. Don't.

Act Three

Act Three: Dominate

The final climb

  1. Hold your course. Don't pull back at the last minute.
  2. Keep Minthara as your partner: the game ends your other romances once you commit.
  3. At the finale, take the ending that gives the Absolute the most power.

Love & Companions

The game is open: any companion can be romanced whatever your character looks like

The Absolute is loyal to one power-seeker. Minthara is the headline: she can be romanced whatever your character looks like.

Our pickWhy they fitAlternates
MaleAstarionHe loves power and cares for himself first. He approved of the raid, and he'll admire a ruthless partner.Gale, if you pass the check to keep him
FemaleMintharaA drow commander who respects strength and nothing else.Lae'zel, who admires the strong
How to win herIn Act Two, once she's recruited and her approval is high enough, she starts a conversation at camp. Let her read your mind. Imagine her as a lover, an ally, a friend or a survivor, and finish with "I want you too." Imagining her as a monster ruins it. She likes cruelty, power and strength, and dislikes compassion for the weak and any mention of Lolth.
She doesn't shareAt high approval she becomes your exclusive partner, and the game ends your other romances. She also refuses threesomes and disapproves of Mizora's night.
