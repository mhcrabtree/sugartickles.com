# Fan Content notice — Baldur's Gate 3 Paths

This folder is **original SugarTickles writing**. It does not adapt bg3.wiki text, so it is not bound by the wiki's
CC BY-NC-SA license (unlike `../baldurs-gate-3/`). Keep it that way: don't paste wiki text here.

## Rules we follow

Larian's [BG3 Fan Content Terms](https://baldursgate3.game/bg3-fan-content-terms/) (updated 31 July 2025) fold in
Wizards of the Coast's [Fan Content Policy](https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy). So:

- **Show the disclaimer** (it's on every page, in Credits): *"[Title] is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the
  Fan Content Policy. Not approved/endorsed by Wizards. Portions of the materials used are property of Wizards of the
  Coast. ©Wizards of the Coast LLC."*
- **No logos.** No D&D, Wizards, Baldur's Gate or Larian logos, and no BG3 key art lockup.
- **No verbatim rules text.** Describe spells and features in our own words. Don't paste descriptions from the game or books.
- **Free to read.** No payments, sign-ups, or email walls. (Ads and donations are allowed by the Wizards policy as long as they
  don't get in the way, but that's a separate decision for the site.)
- **No game video or music** from Wizards. Our own gameplay video on YouTube is fine.
- **No generative AI** prompted or trained on Larian IP.

## Gear and game facts

Item names, locations and class facts are compiled from public guides (AltChar for Act One, Gamer Guides for Acts Two
and Three, bg3.wiki for Patch 8 subclasses and Minthara) and rewritten in our own words. Facts aren't copyrightable,
but wording is: never paste guide text. Locations are unverified until we confirm them in our own playthrough.

The Spells Guide and Easter Egg Guide follow the same rule: facts from bg3.wiki, reworded, never pasted. Spoiler
entries stay inside `<details class="spoiler">` boxes. This folder stays separate from `../baldurs-gate-3/`, whose text
is CC BY-NC-SA and must never be monetized. (The only edit there is two links to these pages in its header.)

The `romance/` guide covers adult encounters (Haarlep, Mizora, Naoise, the Orlith twins, the Emperor). Keep it high-level:
no explicit description, a mature-content banner, and character roles in `<details class="spoiler">` boxes.

Each path's act chapters follow a shared step-by-step route written in our own words (area and quest names are game
facts, the prose is ours). Don't paste text from the wiki or from `../baldurs-gate-3/` into it.

Larian or Wizards can change these terms at any time. Re-check them before adding anything new.
