Your Character

Build The Scholar

A High Elf Abjurer: a tough-ish Wizard with a magic ward. This chapter picks the build, the spells and the order to learn them.

Chapter 1

The Idea

Who the Scholar is, and why Abjuration

The Scholar reads, studies and learns. A Wizard has the biggest spell list in the game, but also the thinnest armor. The School of Abjuration is the best answer for a new player: it gives you a magic ward that soaks damage before your hit points do.

The main trick of a Wizard is that you learn new spells from scrolls. Every scroll you find can be copied into your spellbook. That makes loot a core part of the class, and this page tells you which scrolls to look for.

Patch 8 alternativePatch 8 added Bladesinging, a Wizard who fights with a sword and moves like a dancer. It's a fun choice if you want to be in the thick of things. We built this page around Abjuration for its sturdiness.

Where scrolls come fromCheck every trader that sells scrolls, and loot every caster you defeat. Copying costs 50 gold per spell level, and half that for your own school. A few spells, like Counterspell, Shield and Find Familiar, have no scroll: you learn those by leveling up.

Staying on Target

The Scholar's Playthrough

Read, copy, cast. Here's the plan.

Act One

Act One: Build the Spellbook

Collect scrolls and keep your distance

  1. Recruit Karlach, Lae'zel and Shadowheart.
  2. Cast Mage Armour every morning, and keep Shield ready.
  3. Copy every scroll you find into your spellbook.
  4. Stay behind your fighters. Use cantrips from a distance and save slots for bigger fights.
  5. Meet Gale and borrow ideas.
Recommended: your first big findPick up the Warped Headband of Intellect from Lump the Enlightened in the Blighted Village. It's a headband made for Intelligence casters.
To verify while provingConfirm what the headband does and what it costs.
Act Two

Act Two: Fill the Pages

The shadows hide scrolls

  1. Take the Incandescent Staff from Last Light's quartermaster.
  2. Learn Counterspell and Fireball by level 5.
  3. Keep your ward up: cast an abjuration spell at the start of a fight.

Act Three

Act Three: The Archmage

The biggest spells in the biggest city

  1. Solve the Arcane Barrier puzzle in Ramazith's Tower for Markoheshkir.
  2. Pick up the Cloak of the Weave in the Sorcerer's Vaults.
  3. At the finale, use your best spells and let your party protect you.
