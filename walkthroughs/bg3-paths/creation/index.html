Character Creation, ExplainedCantrips, spells and subclasses

The creation screen asks questions the game never explains: pick four cantrips, pick a subclass, pick a dragon colour. This page says what each one means, how many you get, and which choices you can fix later. If you're following one of our paths, the last table has every path's picks in one place.

The Four Words You Need

What the creation screen is talking about

WordWhat it meansExample
ClassYour job: how you fight and what you're good at. There are twelve.Sorcerer, Fighter, Cleric
SubclassA specialty inside your class. Two Sorcerers with different subclasses play quite differently. Each class picks it at a set level: some at level 1, some at 2 or 3.Shadow Magic Sorcerer, Draconic Bloodline Sorcerer
CantripA small spell you can cast as often as you like, for free. It's your everyday attack or trick.Fire Bolt, Guidance, Eldritch Blast
Spell (a "leveled" spell)A bigger spell that uses up a spell slot. You only have a few slots, and they come back when you rest.Magic Missile, Healing Word, Fireball
"Level" means two thingsYour character level goes from 1 to 12. A spell's level goes from 1 to 6 and says how powerful it is. A level 5 Sorcerer casts level 3 spells, like Fireball. Cantrips are "level 0".

What's a Cantrip?

Your free, unlimited magic

A cantrip is the spell you cast when nothing special is happening. It costs no spell slot, so you can cast one every turn, every fight, forever. For a spellcaster, a damage cantrip is what a sword is to a fighter.

  • Damage cantrips (Fire Bolt, Eldritch Blast, Sacred Flame, Vicious Mockery) are your normal attack. Most of your turns will be one of these.
  • Utility cantrips (Guidance, Mage Hand, Minor Illusion, Light) help outside combat: a bonus on a skill roll, moving an object from afar, a distraction.
  • They get stronger on their own. Damage cantrips roll more dice at character levels 5 and 10. You never have to upgrade them.
  • Most can't be swapped when you level up. Leveling up lets some classes trade a spell, but your cantrips stay. To change a cantrip, rebuild with Withers.
How to pick cantrips when a guide doesn't sayTake one damage cantrip you'll use every turn, one with a different damage type for enemies that resist the first, and one for outside combat. If you get a fourth, take another attack: a close-range one (Shocking Grasp) or one that weakens enemies (Bone Chill, Ray of Frost).
Don't double up with your raceSome races give you a cantrip for free (see Race cantrips below). A Mephistopheles Tiefling already knows Mage Hand, so picking it again from your class wastes a slot.

Subclasses, and Why Some Appear on Day One

When the game asks you to specialize

Every class gets a subclass, but not at the same time. Clerics, Paladins, Sorcerers and Warlocks pick at level 1, so the creation screen shows the choice straight away. Druids and Wizards pick at level 2, and everyone else at level 3. If your path says "subclass at level 3", the creation screen won't show it yet. That's normal: the level-up screen asks later.

Some subclasses ask a second question. A Draconic Bloodline Sorcerer picks a dragon colour, a Cleric picks a god, and a Ranger picks a favoured enemy and a home terrain. Our paths answer those too.

Patch 8 subclassesPatch 8 (April 2025) added a new subclass to every class. Three of our paths use one: Shadow Magic Sorcerer (The Absolute), Hexblade Warlock (The Lone Wanderer) and Drunken Master Monk (The Tipsy Fist). Guides from before 2025 won't mention them.

The Screens That Confuse Everyone

Questions we had, answered

You see…What it means
Sorcerer: "Shadow Magic" or a dragon colour?Those are two different subclasses. Shadow Magic is darkness and survival. Draconic Bloodline makes you pick a dragon colour, and the colour sets a damage type (red, gold and brass are fire). Your path names one; ignore the other. Fire spells like Fire Bolt work for any Sorcerer: the subclass doesn't decide which spells you can learn.
A list of cantrips with a counter (0/4)Pick that many. Use the path's list, or the rule in What's a Cantrip?
Cleric: a list of godsMostly flavour and dialogue. It doesn't change your spells.
Ranger: Favoured Enemy and Natural ExplorerTwo small bonuses, picked at level 1. Each gives a skill, a spell or a resistance. You pick again at levels 6 and 10.
Warlock: "Pact Boon"Not until level 3. Blade gives a summoned weapon, Chain a pet, Tome extra cantrips.
Spells you can't pick, shown greyed outThey're higher level than your slots. They'll unlock as you level.
A skill already ticked and lockedYour race or background already gave it. Pick a different one.
