Chapter 1

The Idea

Who the Charmer is, and why a Rogue

The Charmer steals their way through the game and flirts with everyone worth flirting with, including the famously hard-to-win Shadowheart. A Rogue is the right class: it has the most skill training, can double down on skills like Persuasion and Sleight of Hand, and does huge damage by striking from the shadows.

The skill is staying smooth. This page keeps a ledger so you know what each companion wants to see, and where you're likely to burn a bridge.

Chapter 5

Gear Worth Finding

Light armor, a finesse weapon, and gloves that help

You're built to be fast, not heavy. Look for light armor, a one-handed finesse weapon like a dagger or rapier, a ranged weapon for sneaky openings, and any item that boosts Dexterity, Persuasion or Sleight of Hand. Put your best lockpicks and thieves' tools in a spare pack.

Act One

Act One: Warm Up

Meet everyone and collect your first favors

  1. Recruit Astarion, Gale, Shadowheart and Lae'zel early; the Charmer needs an audience.
  2. Pickpocket at the Emerald Grove, but never the leaders: failing there starts a fight you don't want.
  3. Start with Astarion: he's receptive to flirting, so you can learn how romance scenes work.
  4. Build Shadowheart's trust slowly. Be honest, and don't push on her secret.
  5. Hold a long rest after each big event, and talk to everyone.

Act Two

Act Two: Keep the Plates Spinning

The shadowy lands, with plenty of chances to slip

  1. Use stealth and Persuasion to bypass fights; save your best talk for when it counts.
  2. Check your ledger before every camp scene: who might be upset?
  3. Steal from Moonrise Towers' guards, but only if you can do so unseen.
