Build The Charmer

A Rogue who picks pockets and hearts alike. Quick hands, a quick tongue, and a ledger of who likes what.

Chapter 1

The Idea

Who the Charmer is, and why a Rogue

The Charmer steals their way through the game and flirts with everyone worth flirting with, including the famously hard-to-win Shadowheart. A Rogue is the right class: it has the most skill training, can double down on Persuasion and Sleight of Hand, and does huge damage by striking from the shadows.

The skill is staying smooth. This page keeps a ledger so you know what each companion wants to see, and where you're likely to burn a bridge.

Patch 8 alternativePatch 8 added the Swashbuckler Rogue, a dashing duellist who thrives one-on-one. It's a natural fit for a charmer with a rapier. We built this page around the Thief because it's the best at stealing.

Act One

Act One: Warm Up

Meet everyone and collect your first favors

  1. Recruit Lae'zel, Shadowheart and Gale early; the Charmer needs an audience.
  2. Pickpocket at the Emerald Grove, but never the leaders: failing there starts a fight you don't want.
  3. Deliver the unopened chest to Zarys, then buy the Gloves of Thievery from Brem.
  4. Build Shadowheart's trust slowly. Be honest, and don't push on her secret.
  5. Hold a long rest after each big event, and talk to everyone.
Recommended: your first big findLook inside the stone in the Underdark for Phalar Aluve. It's the best longsword for a Rogue, and it makes your whole party hit harder.

Act Two

Act Two: Keep the Plates Spinning

The shadowy lands, with plenty of chances to slip

  1. Use stealth and Persuasion to bypass fights; save your best talk for when it counts.
  2. Check your ledger before every camp scene: who might be upset?
  3. Pick up the Ring of Shadow from Olivar's quest and walk past guards unseen.

Act Three

Act Three: The Last Dance

Settle your affairs before the finale

  1. Before the finale, decide which relationship is the one you want, and see its scene through.
  2. Don't leave unresolved romances hanging; speak to each partner before the final fight.
Minthara won't shareIf you romance Minthara, she takes the partner slot and ends your other romances. Skip her unless you mean it.
